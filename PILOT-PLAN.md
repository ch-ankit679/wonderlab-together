# WonderLab Together — classroom pilot plan (5.0)

This plan evaluates the product; it is not evidence that the product has already improved learning.

## Pilot question

Can the six-lesson multimedia pathway help students explain the chapter concepts more clearly, and can it do so without increasing total teacher workload?

## Suggested pilot

- One class using one complete chapter pathway.
- Split the six lessons over one or two periods to suit the timetable; do not rush every simulation just to fit a single period.
- Use a short pre-lesson concept explanation task and a parallel post-lesson task aligned to the lesson objectives.
- Where practical, repeat a short check several days later to see whether students retain the ideas.
- Record preparation, setup, delivery and follow-up time.

## What to collect

1. **Starting understanding:** short prompts that ask students to explain or apply the target concepts.
2. **Lesson evidence:** concept-check outcomes and which lessons need another look.
3. **Application:** Wonder League answers, explanations and recurring misconceptions.
4. **Retention:** a brief delayed check, where feasible.
5. **Participation:** observe whether different students contribute and operate the shared screen, not only the most confident students.
6. **Workload:** minutes spent preparing, setting up, running, reviewing and recording the session compared with the usual approach.

## Interpret results carefully

- A correct in-app answer is not proof of durable understanding.
- Team-level results are not individual learning results.
- A pre/post change in one class is descriptive evidence, not proof the platform caused the change.
- Where practical, compare with the school's usual method or a similar class and record differences in prior knowledge, lesson time and teaching conditions.
- Do not collect individual names or sensitive student information in this prototype.

## Decision after the pilot

Identify concepts that students still find difficult, revise unclear explanations or models, and remove any step that creates friction. Expand to other chapters only after checking curriculum alignment and reviewing classroom evidence.
